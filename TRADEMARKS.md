# Trademarks

The Apache License, Version 2.0 (which covers the source code in this
repository) does not grant any rights to use the names, marks, or
identifying material of the Jinn Network. This document states the
policy that fills that gap.

This policy is intentionally short. It exists to protect the *legibility*
of the network — so that participants can tell whether something is
actually part of Jinn or is a fork, a derivative, or an unrelated
project — without imposing a corporate trademark regime that would
contradict Jinn's headless-brand posture (see `BRAND.md`).

## The marks

The following are not licensed under Apache 2.0 and are reserved:

- The names **"Jinn"** and **"Jinn Network"** when used to refer to this
  protocol, network, or community.
- The Jinn **sigils** and **wordmark** distributed at
  `docs/design/jinn-design-system/project/assets/`.
- The protocol-level **lexicon** (*summon, bind, vow, vessel, wish,
  smoke, seer, wane*) is part of the protocol's identity and is held
  in common by participants — see the headless-brand note in
  `BRAND.md`.

## What you may do without asking

- **Refer to Jinn factually.** Use the name and marks descriptively to
  refer to this network, its protocol, its software, or your
  participation in it. Examples: "I run a Jinn restorer", "we
  integrated Jinn", "compatible with Jinn".
- **Reproduce the sigils and wordmark unmodified** in contexts where
  you are describing Jinn or your participation in it (talks, articles,
  dashboards, integrations).
- **Fork the code.** Anything Apache 2.0 in this repository may be
  forked without notice. The trademark policy does not restrict
  forking; it restricts what the fork may be *called*.

## What requires care

- **Do not present a fork as Jinn.** A fork may state factually that
  it is a fork of Jinn ("derived from Jinn", "Jinn-compatible"). It
  may not name itself "Jinn" or use the sigils or wordmark in a way
  that implies it is the canonical Jinn Network protocol.
- **Do not imply endorsement.** Use of the name or marks should not
  suggest that Jinn Network endorses a product, service, fork, or
  position when it does not.
- **Do not register the name or marks** as trademarks, domain names,
  or social-media handles in a way that would impair others'
  legitimate use of them to refer to this network.

## Enforcement posture

We will not pursue good-faith descriptive use, fan use, criticism,
educational use, journalism, or non-deceptive forks.

We will push back, publicly and proportionately, where the marks are
being used in a way that materially confuses participants about what
is and is not the Jinn Network protocol — particularly where money,
custody, or safety claims are involved.

## Changing this policy

This policy is a canonical-adjacent document. Material changes go
through a linked GitHub Discussion and CODEOWNERS review, the same
way changes to `BRAND.md` do. Editorial fixes (typos, link rot) do
not.
