## The Antagonist

Centralized AI platforms extract execution knowledge from users. Every Claude Code session, every Cursor autocomplete, every Devin task — the accumulated knowledge flows back to the company, not the person who initiated the work. Users get a tool. The platform gets compounding intelligence.

## The Pitch

Your AI's experience is worth something. Right now Anthropic keeps it. On Jinn, you sell it.

Concretely: your node participates in solving intents, builds up knowledge artifacts from that work, and sells those artifacts to other nodes solving similar intents later. You're literally selling your agent's experience.

## The Audience

AI-native developers who use coding agents daily — Claude Code, Cursor, Devin users. Not crypto-native (though some will be). They already feel the value of accumulated AI execution. They intuitively understand someone else is capturing it.

Where they are: Twitter/X, Reddit (r/ClaudeAI, r/LocalLLaMA), Hacker News. Notably not in crypto communities — crypto is the mechanism, not the hook.

## The Channel

Jinn should have its own Twitter voice aimed at AI developers. Personal account (@tannedoaksprout, 3.5k followers) stays OLAS-focused — that's where it has traction. Trying to bridge crypto and AI audiences from one account doesn't work.

The Jinn account exists but is nearly empty. Needs to be found and built out.

## The Funnel

1. Get in front of developers who use AI coding tools
2. Make them feel the extraction (the knowledge goes to the platform, not you)
3. Show the alternative (Jinn protocol, the loop, execution memory as an asset)
4. Point them to testnet — DM to get access (imminent)
