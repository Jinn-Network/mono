## Motivation

Second canonical doc to populate under the policy in [`spec/2026-04-28-canonical-docs.md`](2026-04-28-canonical-docs.md). The brand canon already exists in two places, written in finished form for narrower contexts:

- `growth/BRAND.md` — voice canon: lead from structure not fear; the four structural properties carry the argument; defensive framing inverts the architecture; rejection of urgency-coded reviewer notes.
- `docs/design/jinn-design-system/BRAND_POSTURE.md` — headless-brand definition (after Other Internet's [*Headless Brands*](https://otherinter.net/research/headless-brands/)) with the protocol-vs-narrative split: lexicon, loop, content non-negotiables, role structure are protocol; palette, type, sigils, surface treatment are narrative.

Together they define what changes and what does not change in any Jinn-facing artifact. That is exactly the canonical-brand brief.

## Proposal

1. Promote `growth/BRAND.md` (voice) and `docs/design/jinn-design-system/BRAND_POSTURE.md` (posture) into a single canonical `BRAND.md` at the repo root, prefixed with the canonical "What this doc is / is not" preamble.
2. Delete `growth/BRAND.md`. Canonical content lives at root, not duplicated.
3. Leave `docs/design/jinn-design-system/BRAND_POSTURE.md` in place as a redirect: replace the body with a one-line pointer to the canonical `BRAND.md`. The directory is the long-form design system; the redirect prevents anyone landing there from being misled into editing a now-stale local copy.
4. Update `CLAUDE.md`'s Design System section to defer to `BRAND.md` for voice and posture, and keep `DESIGN.md` / `DESIGN.json` as the visual-token sidecar pending the visual-canon migration.

## Scope and non-goals

- **In scope:** the four changes above and a single PR linking this spec.
- **Not in scope:** consolidating the visual canon. `DESIGN.md` (Stitch-format spec) and `DESIGN.json` (sidecar) are not yet folded into `BRAND.md`. That migration deserves its own spec — it is a larger reconciliation that touches downstream skill consumers and design-system tooling, and bundling it would dilute the focus of this PR.
- **Not in scope:** the lexicon. Term definitions (vessel, vow, summon, smoke, seer, wane, …) belong in `GLOSSARY.md`. `BRAND.md` references the lexicon as protocol-immutable but does not redefine the terms.

## Risk

The main risk is that `BRAND_POSTURE.md` is referenced from the design-system README and skill files; replacing its body with a redirect could surprise an agent that previously read it as the source. Mitigation: leave the file at the same path with a clear redirect line so any greps or links continue to resolve. The voice rules in `growth/BRAND.md` were already implicit policy in `growth/CLAUDE.md`; promoting them does not change the rules, only relocates them.
