## Motivation

`THESIS.md` is the first canonical doc to be populated under the policy in [`spec/2026-04-28-canonical-docs.md`](2026-04-28-canonical-docs.md). The thesis content already exists at `growth/THESIS.md` — written for the growth context but stating, in finished form, why Jinn exists, the bet, the non-goals, and what we are explicitly *not*. That is exactly the canonical-thesis brief.

`PRODUCT.md` at the repo root has been functioning as a partial substitute (brand register, audiences, anti-references, strategic principles). The canonical-docs rollout designates `PRODUCT.md` as superseded by `THESIS.md`, with brand-register material destined for `BRAND.md` in a later follow-up. The brand-register content in `PRODUCT.md` is also stale relative to the thesis (different framings, narrower scope) and is better re-derived from `BRAND.md` than partially merged here.

## Proposal

1. Promote the body of `growth/THESIS.md` to the canonical `THESIS.md` at the repo root, prefixed with the canonical "What this doc is / is not" preamble.
2. Delete `growth/THESIS.md` — canonical content lives at root, not duplicated.
3. Delete `PRODUCT.md` — superseded per [`spec/2026-04-28-canonical-docs.md`](2026-04-28-canonical-docs.md). Strategic-principles and brand-register content will be reconciled into `BRAND.md` in its own follow-up; doing it here would conflate two canon migrations.
4. Update `CLAUDE.md`'s Design System section to drop the `PRODUCT.md` reference (the migration note already in place from the bootstrap PR continues to apply).

## Scope and non-goals

- **In scope:** the four file changes above and a single PR linking this spec.
- **Not in scope:** populating `BRAND.md`, rewriting the thesis prose, reconciling lexicon (lives in `GLOSSARY.md` later), or migrating the rest of `growth/`.

## Risk

The main risk is content drift between `growth/THESIS.md`-as-source and the operational growth context that previously read it in place. Mitigation: any growth-only tactical material was already in `growth/CLAUDE.md` and other `growth/` docs; the thesis itself is a strategic-canon artifact and was always meant to live above the `growth/` directory.
