## 1. What veAERO is

Aerodrome is a ve(3,3) DEX on Base, built by the Velodrome team and forked from the Solidly lineage. AERO is the emissions token. Locking AERO in the VotingEscrow contract mints a **veAERO position as an ERC-721 NFT** (not a fungible balance), with a lock term up to four years. Voting power scales with amount × remaining lock time and decays linearly as the lock runs down, unless the position is set to permanent/max-lock, which holds power flat. Each weekly epoch, veAERO holders vote to direct the coming week's AERO emissions across liquidity pool gauges. In exchange, a voter receives the trading fees generated by the pools they voted for, plus any external incentives ("bribes") deposited on those gauges, both pro-rata to their share of that gauge's vote. There is also a rebase paid to lockers to partially offset emission dilution. The design point: emissions are allocated by the people with the longest-dated exposure to the token, and those people are paid in the revenue their allocation produces.

## 2. Live status and chains

Live on **Base** since late August 2023, and Base only — veAERO is not deployed cross-chain. The sibling deployment is Velodrome/veVELO on Optimism (and Velodrome later expanded to other Superchain networks), but that is a separate token and separate contracts.

## 3. Core contracts

Roles I am confident about (Velodrome V2 architecture, which Aerodrome inherits):

- **`Aero`** — the ERC-20 emissions token.
- **`VotingEscrow`** — the veAERO ERC-721. Holds locked AERO, tracks the decay curve, handles lock creation, extension, merge, split, permanent lock, delegation.
- **`Voter`** — the vote registry and gauge registry. Records votes per tokenId, maps pool → gauge, whitelists reward tokens, creates gauges, and pushes weekly emissions to gauges via `distribute`.
- **`Minter`** — mints the weekly emission and splits it between the Voter (for gauges) and the RewardsDistributor (for the rebase). Advanced by `updatePeriod()` at each epoch flip.
- **`RewardsDistributor`** — the anti-dilution rebase to veAERO holders.
- **`Gauge`** (one per pool) — LPs stake here; streams AERO to stakers.
- **`FeesVotingReward`** — per-gauge contract holding that pool's trading fees, claimable by voters.
- **`BribeVotingReward`** — per-gauge contract holding externally deposited incentives, claimable by voters.
- **`PoolFactory` / `Pool` / `Router`** — the AMM itself (stable and volatile pool types).
- **`GaugeFactory`, `VotingRewardsFactory`, `FactoryRegistry`** — deployment plumbing.
- **Slipstream** (`CLFactory`, `CLPool`, `CLGauge`, `NonfungiblePositionManager`) — the concentrated-liquidity system added later, gauged through the same Voter.

## 4. Mechanics: lock 1,000 AERO, then vote a gauge

**Step 1 — approve.**
`AERO.approve(votingEscrow, 1000e18)`

**Step 2 — create the lock.**
`VotingEscrow.createLock(uint256 _value, uint256 _lockDuration)`
- `_value = 1000e18`
- `_lockDuration` in seconds, capped at four years (126,144,000 s), and rounded down internally to a weekly epoch boundary.
- Returns the `tokenId` of the new veAERO NFT, minted to `msg.sender`. Use `createLockFor(value, duration, recipient)` to mint to another address.

Optional: `VotingEscrow.lockPermanent(tokenId)` to convert to a non-decaying max-power lock. Reversible via `unlockPermanent`, which restarts the four-year decay.

Check the resulting power with `VotingEscrow.balanceOfNFT(tokenId)`.

**Step 3 — vote.**
`Voter.vote(uint256 _tokenId, address[] _poolVote, uint256[] _weights)`
- `_poolVote` takes **pool addresses, not gauge addresses** — the Voter resolves pool → gauge internally.
- `_weights` are relative, not percentages; `[100]` for a single pool casts your full power. `[75, 25]` across two pools splits it 3:1.
- The caller must own or be approved on the tokenId.
- Voting is disabled during a short distribution window at the epoch flip (Thursday 00:00 UTC; I recall the window as one hour, medium confidence).
- Voting again within the same epoch replaces the previous vote. `Voter.reset(tokenId)` clears votes and is required before withdrawing or, in some paths, transferring the NFT.

**Step 4 — claim, the following epoch.**
- Fees: `Voter.claimFees(address[] _fees, address[][] _tokens, uint256 _tokenId)`, or directly on each `FeesVotingReward.getReward(tokenId, tokens[])`.
- Incentives: `Voter.claimBribes(...)` with the same shape against `BribeVotingReward`.
- Rebase: `RewardsDistributor.claim(tokenId)`, which compounds into the lock rather than paying out liquid.

One thing I am unsure of: whether votes automatically persist into the next epoch or must be recast weekly. My recollection is that Aerodrome requires recasting each epoch (with `poke` used to refresh a decayed position's weight), but I would not act on that without checking.
