## Rules section — add one line

Append to the Rules block:

```
- Deep block (11–13:00) is reserved for highest-cognition Jinn work only:
  teaching artefacts (threads, essays, Looms) and understanding conversations
  (listening, thesis pressure-testing). Offer-making, DMs, replies, and
  outbound scheduling are afternoon work — they're cheap when cognition is
  fresh and feel productive, which is exactly why they crowd out the
  compounding work if allowed in the deep block.
```

## Why this change

The Jinn growth bottleneck is operator count. Operator count is a recruiting
problem, and the highest-leverage recruiting action is public teaching — that
is where second-degree operators surface from. Teaching requires the cleanest
cognitive window of the day. DMs and offer admin feel like progress but are
near-zero compounding; they belong in the post-lunch dip where their fitness-
to-task is correct.
