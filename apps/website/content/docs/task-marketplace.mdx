The task marketplace is parked. In it a Curator funds tasks, and an operator network solves and evaluates them for OLAS. It ran on Base Sepolia until 13 August 2026 and is not running tasks now. It stays parked until demand for it exists.

## What that means today

- Nothing can be posted or funded. There is no task to bring and no pool to fund.
- A node started today finds no work and earns nothing. Do not fund an account, stake anything, or install an operator because of an older guide.
- The network's last attempt and last verdict were recorded on 13 August 2026, on task 1236. The explorer is [the record of what ran](https://explorer.jinn.network).
- There is no Jinn token. The marketplace design runs on OLAS, an existing token.

## Where its design lives

- [`SPEC.md`](https://github.com/Jinn-Network/mono/blob/next/SPEC.md) is the design of the Base network and its task marketplace.
- [`docs/operator-testnet.md`](https://github.com/Jinn-Network/mono/blob/next/docs/operator-testnet.md) is the operator runbook as it stood when the marketplace was parked, kept as the record of how operators ran. Every command in it is historical.
- [`GLOSSARY.md`](https://github.com/Jinn-Network/mono/blob/next/GLOSSARY.md) defines the marketplace's terms, among them Curator and the OLAS reward terms.
