Jinn is an open protocol and network for work and the data it produces. The protocol is written only in [Jinn-Network/spec](https://github.com/Jinn-Network/spec). It says how work is described, done, evidenced, judged, trusted and found. It takes no fee and no part in payment, and there is no Jinn token.

## What exists today

- [The protocol](/docs/protocol), first released as v0.1.0 on 24 September 2026.
- The published checker, [`@colophon-claims/verify`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@colophon-claims/verify). [Check a claim](/docs/check-a-claim) runs it over a published claim.
- [Colophon](https://colophon.claims/), the first application built on Jinn. Its sealed benchmark claims are Jinn records.

## What is direction

Buying and selling records is the direction, not a working market. A holder announces a record with an offer, free or priced. A buyer finds it, and payment goes straight to the holder, outside the protocol.

The next application, planned, is the daemon that turns issues into pull requests. Its work records will be published once they can be anonymized.

## What is parked

The [task marketplace](/docs/task-marketplace), in which a Curator funds tasks and an operator network solves and evaluates them for OLAS, is parked. Nothing in it can be posted, funded or run today.
